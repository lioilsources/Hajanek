## uvod
[soft] Lie down whichever way you like best.
On your side, or on your back.
[pause 4s]
Your blanket hugs you, warm as a nest.
[pause 4s]
Imagine a little balloon in your tummy.
[pause 2s]
Breathe in, and the balloon slowly fills.
[pause 3s]
Breathe out, and the balloon gets small again.
[pause 4s]
Once more. The balloon grows…
[pause 3s]
And quietly shrinks.
[pause 5s]
Your shoulders drop.
Your arms go loose and lie still.
[pause 3s]
Your legs sink slowly into the bed.
[pause 3s]
Your eyelids are heavy, so heavy.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about Sleeping Beauty.
[pause 3s]
It is about a castle with the best sleep in the world.
[pause 4s]
Imagine you are standing in a castle garden.
It is a warm summer evening.
[pause 3s]
Crickets chirp softly in the grass.
Chirp… chirp…
[pause 3s]
On a bench under a rose bush sits a princess.
Her dress is as pink as a wild rose.
Her name is Briar Rose.
[pause 2s]
She moves over to make room for you.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] Long ago, a king and queen lived in this castle.
They wished for a little baby.
[pause 3s]
One spring, a baby girl was born.
They named her Briar Rose.
[pause 4s]
The fairies came to her cradle.
Their voices were like little bells.
[pause 3s]
One wished her a kind heart.
One, a lovely voice.
And one, a happy laugh.
[pause 4s]
Then the youngest fairy leaned over the cradle.
"I give you the gift of sweet sleep," she said.
"On just the right evening, the whole castle will sleep with you."
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Many years went by, and Briar Rose grew up.
She loves to smell every rose in the garden.
[pause 4s]
Tonight is just the right evening.
The sun sets, and the castle glows gold.
[pause 4s]
Briar Rose leads you up the tower stairs.
Step by step. Slowly.
[pause 4s]
In the tower is a little room with a round window.
On the bed lies a fluffy quilt.
[pause 3s]
Briar Rose sits on the bed and yawns.
Aaah.
"I am so wonderfully sleepy," she says softly.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
And at that moment, the whole castle yawns.
[pause 3s]
In the kitchen, the cook yawns.
He puts down his wooden spoon.
[pause 3s]
The kitchen boy falls asleep on a sack of flour.
[pause 4s]
The gardener falls asleep under an apple tree.
His straw hat slides down over his eyes.
[pause 4s]
In the stables, the horses fall asleep.
The dogs curl up into balls.
The doves on the roof tuck their heads under their wings.
[pause 4s]
The king and queen fall asleep on their thrones.
Hand in hand, on soft cushions.
[pause 5s]
The flies on the wall fall asleep.
Even the fire grows quiet and dozes.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Around the castle, a wild rose bush begins to grow.
Slowly, leaf by leaf.
[pause 4s]
It grows and grows.
It covers the whole castle like a green quilt.
[pause 4s]
Wild roses bloom on the bush.
They are pale pink and smell sweet.
[pause 4s]
In every flower, a little bee is sleeping.
Snuggled in the petals.
[pause 5s]
Here everyone will sleep for a hundred years.
That is a long, long, lovely sleep.
[pause 4s]
Many years from now, a young prince will come.
The roses will part for him like a curtain.
And everyone will be wonderfully rested.
[pause 4s]
But that will be a long time from now.
Now it is time to sleep.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]
