## uvod
[soft] Now it is time to rest.
Lie down and snuggle into your blanket.
[pause 4s]
Your blanket is like a warm meadow.
Your pillow is like a soft little hill.
[pause 4s]
Breathe in slowly, like a gentle breeze.
[pause 3s]
And breathe out, like a breeze floating away. Ffff…
[pause 4s]
Once more. The breeze comes in…
[pause 3s]
And the breeze goes out. Ffff…
[pause 5s]
Your arms are heavy, like two warm stones.
[pause 3s]
Your legs lie still, calm and warm.
[pause 3s]
Your head sinks deeper into the pillow.
Your eyelids slowly, slowly close.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about three little pigs.
[pause 3s]
It is about the wind, little houses, and one sleepy wolf.
In the end, everyone sleeps warm.
[pause 4s]
Imagine a green meadow.
The grass ripples in the wind like the sea.
[pause 3s]
Whoosh, sings the wind.
[pause 3s]
In the meadow stand three little pink pigs.
They have round tummies and curly tails.
[pause 2s]
They grunt happily. Oink, oink.
And they take you along.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] The little pigs are all grown up now.
Their mother strokes their snouts and says,
"It is time to build your own little houses."
[pause 4s]
The first little pig finds a pile of straw.
The straw is golden and smells of sunshine.
[pause 3s]
He builds a little house out of it.
Rustle… rustle… and it is done.
[pause 3s]
The second little pig gathers sticks in the woods.
She lays them side by side.
Tap… tap… and the little house is ready.
[pause 3s]
She brings in green moss.
It makes a lovely soft bed.
[pause 4s]
The third little pig brings bricks.
Brick on brick. Slowly and carefully.
[pause 3s]
He builds a house with strong walls and a fireplace.
Gray smoke rises from the chimney.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
In the evening, the wind picks up.
It hums across the meadow.
Shhh… shhh…
[pause 4s]
The wind whistles through the straw house.
The straws sway and rustle softly.
[pause 3s]
"It is drafty in here," says the first little pig.
He takes his pillow and goes to his sister.
[pause 4s]
The wind whistles through the stick house too.
The sticks tap against each other.
[pause 3s]
So the two little pigs walk to the brick house.
Each one carries a pillow.
[pause 4s]
The third little pig opens the door wide.
"Come in. It is warm in here."
[pause 4s]
A fire crackles in the fireplace.
Crackle… crackle…
The little pigs sit on the rug and warm their hooves.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Soon there is a soft little knock at the door.
Tap… tap…
At the door stands a sleepy wolf.
He is chilly, and as shaggy as an old blanket.
[pause 4s]
The wolf gives a long, long yawn.
Whooo…
[pause 3s]
"Good evening, little pigs," he rumbles.
"I walked in the wind all day.
I would love to warm up here for a while."
[pause 4s]
The little pigs smile at each other.
And they make room for the wolf by the fire.
[pause 4s]
The second little pig brings him a blanket.
He wraps himself up to his ears.
[pause 4s]
Then he curls up in front of the fireplace.
He stretches his paws toward the fire.
"Aaah, that feels good," he hums happily.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
The third little pig makes herbal tea.
It smells of mint and honey.
Everyone gets a full mug. Even the wolf.
[pause 3s]
The wolf blows gently on his tea.
Ffff…
[pause 5s]
Outside, the wind still blows a little.
But in the brick house, it is quiet and warm.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]
