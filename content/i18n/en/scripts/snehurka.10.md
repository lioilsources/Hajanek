## uvod
[soft] It is time for sleep.
Lie down any way you like best.
[pause 3s]
Your blanket keeps you warm, toes to chin.
[pause 4s]
Slowly breathe in.
Your shoulders rise a little.
[pause 3s]
And breathe out, as if blowing on a candle.
So gently that it stays lit.
[pause 4s]
Once more. A long, calm breath in.
[pause 3s]
And a gentle breath out. The flame only flickers.
[pause 5s]
Your arms lie heavy on the blanket.
Your legs are heavy and warm.
[pause 3s]
Your eyelids are closing.
Lightly, like a snowflake drifting down.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about Snow White.
It is a calm story about a little mountain cottage.
[pause 4s]
Imagine a forest below the mountains.
The evening air smells of pine.
[pause 4s]
A girl walks between the trees. Her hair is black as coal.
Her cheeks are red as rosehips.
[pause 2s]
That is Snow White.
[pause 3s]
She takes your hand. Her hand is gentle and warm.
You walk together, step by step.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] Snow White walks through the forest, over the seven hills.
The forest is quiet. Now and then a little bird chirps.
[pause 4s]
The moss is soft underfoot.
It smells of mushrooms and blueberries.
[pause 4s]
A deer is grazing in a clearing.
She looks up, then calmly goes on grazing.
[pause 4s]
Behind the spruces stands a tiny cottage.
It has green shutters and a low wooden door.
[pause 3s]
Snow White knocks. Knock, knock.
The door is open a crack. The owners are still out at work.
[pause 4s]
Inside, everything is tiny.
[pause 2s]
A little table with seven plates, seven spoons, and seven cups.
[pause 3s]
Seven little beds stand along the wall.
Each has a quilt of a different color.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
There are a few crumbs on the floor.
Snow White takes the broom and sweeps. Swish… swish…
[pause 4s]
Then she lights a fire in the stove. Crackle… crackle…
Soon the cottage is warm.
[pause 4s]
She puts a pot of soup on the stove.
It smells of carrots and herbs.
[pause 4s]
Snow White fluffs all seven pillows.
One by one. Slowly.
[pause 5s]
Then she sits by the stove.
She is pleasantly tired, and she closes her eyes.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Outside, it is slowly getting dark.
The birds settle into their nests.
[pause 4s]
Singing comes from the forest.
The seven little miners are coming home.
[pause 3s]
Each one carries a tiny lantern.
The lights sway like fireflies.
[pause 4s]
They sing softly, in deep little voices.
Hmm… hmm… hmm…
[pause 4s]
Each one wears a different cap.
Red, blue, green, yellow, brown, and gray.
And the smallest one has a cap with a pom-pom.
[pause 5s]
The miners open the door in surprise.
"Someone has tidied up. And cooked soup."
[pause 4s]
Snow White is dozing by the stove.
The miners speak very softly.
[pause 4s]
Snow White opens her eyes and smiles.
"I am Snow White. I found your cottage on my walk."
[pause 3s]
"Stay with us," say the miners.
"It is warm here, with plenty of room."
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
They all sit down at the table.
They eat soup, and bread and butter.
The spoons clink softly. Clink… clink…
[pause 5s]
The miners tell of stones that sparkle like stars.
[pause 4s]
The smallest miner is falling asleep over his plate.
His head nods, and his cap slips over his eyes.
[pause 3s]
The others smile quietly.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]
