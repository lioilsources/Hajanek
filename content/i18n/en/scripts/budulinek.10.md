## uvod
[soft] Snuggle into your blanket.
Curl up like a little kitten.
[pause 3s]
Your head rests on a soft pillow.
[pause 4s]
Breathe in through your nose, like smelling fresh bread.
[pause 3s]
And breathe out slowly, like cooling warm soup.
[pause 4s]
Once more. A slow breath in.
[pause 3s]
And a long, slow breath out.
[pause 5s]
Your arms are heavy and warm.
Your legs are heavy and still.
[pause 3s]
Your forehead is smooth.
Your cheeks are soft.
[pause 3s]
Your eyelids are heavy.
They close by themselves, like a flower at night.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about Budulínek.
[pause 2s]
It is a calm story about a little boy and a fox.
The fox is kind and playful.
[pause 4s]
Imagine a village at the edge of the woods.
There is a cottage with a straw roof.
[pause 2s]
Sunflowers bloom in the garden.
[pause 3s]
On the bench sits a little boy with round cheeks.
That is Budulínek.
[pause 2s]
He swings his legs and smiles at you.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] Budulínek lives with Grandpa and Grandma.
Grandma bakes sweet buns.
And Grandpa plays the fiddle.
[pause 4s]
One morning, Grandpa and Grandma go out to the field.
"Play nicely, Budulínek. We will be back soon," says Grandma.
"Your porridge is on the table."
[pause 4s]
Budulínek eats his porridge.
It is sweet and warm, with a little butter.
[pause 3s]
The clock on the wall goes tick… tock… tick… tock…
[pause 4s]
Outside, the hens cluck. Cluck… cluck…
The cat purrs on the warm doorstep.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Something rustles under the window.
It is a little red fox.
She has a bushy tail with a white tip.
[pause 3s]
The fox is curious. She peeks in.
[pause 3s]
"Budulínek, open the door," she says. "Come and play with me.
I will give you a ride on my tail, all the way to our den."
[pause 4s]
Budulínek opens the door.
The fox bows and swishes her tail.
[pause 3s]
Budulínek sits on her tail.
It is soft as a quilt.
[pause 4s]
And off they go through the woods. Hop… hop… hop…
Nice and slow, so the ride is comfy.
[pause 4s]
Wild strawberries smell sweet along the way.
A jay on a branch watches them go by.
[pause 5s]
Three little fox cubs are playing by the den.
They roll pine cones across the moss.
[pause 3s]
Budulínek plays with them.
They all laugh together.
[pause 4s]
The den is cozy.
It is full of dry leaves and moss.
[pause 3s]
The cubs show Budulínek their treasures.
A shiny pebble, a bird feather, and a pine cone that looks like a hedgehog.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Grandpa and Grandma come home from the field.
The bowl is empty, and little fox prints lead from the door.
[pause 3s]
"Budulínek went out to play," says Grandma with a smile.
"Let us go and find him."
[pause 4s]
Grandpa takes his fiddle.
Grandma takes a basket of buns.
They follow the little fox prints into the woods.
[pause 3s]
The woods smell of pine and mushrooms.
[pause 4s]
At the den, Grandpa plays.
The fiddle sings soft and sweet.
[pause 3s]
And Grandma sings along,
"We play, we play so softly, good night, little foxes."
[pause 4s]
One cub peeks out of the den. Then another. Then a third.
And last of all, the mother fox with Budulínek.
[pause 3s]
They all dance around Grandpa.
Slowly, round and round.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Grandma hands out the buns.
They smell of sweet cheese and plum jam.
The cubs nibble and lick their whiskers.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]

## dojezd
[slow] It is evening. Everyone walks home together.
The fox walks with them to the cottage.
[pause 5s]
Budulínek holds Grandma's hand.
Grandpa plays softly.
[pause 6s]
At the gate, the fox waves her tail.
And she goes back to her cubs in the den.
[pause 6s]
Grandma tucks Budulínek into bed.
[pause 7s]
[whisper] A cricket chirps outside the window. Chirp… chirp…
[pause 7s]
Budulínek is asleep.
