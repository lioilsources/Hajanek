Hajánek is an app for the last ten minutes of the day. Your child hears a familiar fairy tale retold as a bedtime meditation: first they settle in and slow their breathing, then walk slowly along the forest path with Little Red Riding Hood, and at the end the characters fall asleep with them. Underneath, the sea, rain or a music box plays softly, and keeps playing for a while after the tale ends.

The app and its tales are in Czech, read by your phone's Czech voice. It is for children aged about three to eight.

TALES WITH NOTHING TO BE AFRAID OF
Every tale follows the same calm path of about ten minutes: an introduction (blanket, pillow, breathing in and out), the way into the tale, the story, and a wind-down where the voice slows and softens. Everything scary is gone: the wolf eats no one and frightens no one. New tales are added to the catalog and the app downloads them by itself.

JUKEBOX: NATURE SOUNDS AND LULLABIES
The jukebox plays without a tale too, all night if you like. Mix up to three layers, each with its own volume: sea, rain on the roof, a brook, a fireplace, wind in the trees, a night meadow, pink and brown noise, plus slow lullaby loops (music box, piano, harp, guitar, chimes). Every sound is in the app and plays offline.

BEDTIME RITUAL AND SLEEP TIMER
First only the background plays, then the tale begins, then the background fades out slowly. The timer stops after 5 to 60 minutes or at the end of the tale, and never cuts off abruptly.

DARK, NO ADS, NO ACCOUNT
The whole app is dark with large buttons. No ads, no sign-up, no third-party analytics; anonymous usage statistics can be turned off.
