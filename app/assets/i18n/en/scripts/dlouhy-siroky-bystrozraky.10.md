## uvod
[soft] It is evening, and you are lying in bed.
Your blanket holds you like a warm hug.
[pause 3s]
Your head sinks softly into the pillow.
[pause 4s]
Breathe in, like smelling a flower.
[pause 3s]
And breathe out, like blowing on a dandelion.
Whooo…
[pause 4s]
Smell the flower once more.
[pause 3s]
And blow gently on the dandelion.
The fluff floats far away.
[pause 5s]
Your shoulders drop.
Your arms lie beside you, heavy and warm.
[pause 3s]
Your legs are heavy, like two soft pillows.
[pause 3s]
Your eyelids are heavy.
Slowly, they close.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about a prince and three special friends.
Their names are Long, Broad, and Sharpsight.
[pause 3s]
It is a quiet and cheerful story.
Everyone in it is friends.
[pause 4s]
Imagine an old road between the fields.
The air smells of hay.
[pause 3s]
Beside you stands a young prince in a blue cloak.
He has kind eyes and a gentle smile.
[pause 3s]
He nods to you.
"Come, let us go together," he says softly.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] The prince is going to find a princess.
She lives in a magic castle beyond the hills.
[pause 2s]
In that castle, everything is always dozing.
[pause 4s]
By the road, a man stretches, as tall as a fir tree.
"I am Long," he says. "I can reach the clouds."
[pause 3s]
One step, and he is across the meadow.
[pause 4s]
On a stump sits a man round as a barrel.
[pause 2s]
"I am Broad. I can grow as wide as a pond."
Broad laughs, and his tummy jiggles.
[pause 4s]
By a brook stands a man with a cloth over his eyes.
"I am Sharpsight. I see everything, even beyond nine mountains.
This cloth lets my eyes rest."
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
You all walk on. Long takes long steps.
Broad waddles like a duck.
[pause 4s]
Beyond the woods stands the castle.
Every window glows.
[pause 2s]
Horses and dogs sleep in the courtyard.
[pause 4s]
In the doorway stands the old lord of the castle.
He has a long white beard and a nightcap.
He gives a long, sweet yawn.
[pause 5s]
"Welcome," he says kindly. "The princess is here.
She just loves to play hide-and-seek.
Play with her for three evenings, then she will go with you."
[pause 5s]
That evening, everyone sits by the fire.
The fire crackles softly. Crackle… crackle…
[pause 3s]
Apples bake by the fire and smell of cinnamon.
[pause 4s]
The princess smiles and goes to hide.
Sharpsight lifts his cloth a tiny bit.
"I see her. She is an acorn on a big oak in the woods."
[pause 3s]
Long reaches over the forest and gently picks it.
In his hand, it turns back into the princess.
She laughs softly.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
On the second evening, the princess hides again.
[pause 3s]
"She is a sparkly pebble on a high cliff."
[pause 2s]
Long stretches up and brings it down.
It turns into the princess, and she laughs softly.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
On the third evening, the princess hides farthest of all.
"She is a gold ring in a shell, deep in the sea."
[pause 3s]
Broad bends to the sea and sips. Gulp… gulp… gulp…
[pause 3s]
His tummy is round as a drum. And he hiccups a little.
[pause 4s]
Long reaches down and lifts out the shell.
Then Broad slowly pours the sea back, so the fish stay asleep.
[pause 4s]
The ring turns into the princess.
She smiles and gives the prince her hand.
The old lord nods happily.
"There. Now at last I can go to sleep."
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]

## dojezd
[slow] In the castle, the lights go out. One by one.
[pause 5s]
The old lord sleeps in his chair.
Long curls up into a ball. A very long ball.
[pause 6s]
Broad's tummy rises like a little hill.
Sharpsight pulls the cloth over his eyes.
[pause 6s]
The prince and the princess hold hands and close their eyes.
[pause 7s]
[whisper] The fire slowly fades.
[pause 7s]
The whole castle is asleep.
[pause 8s]
And Hajánek is already passing by.
Quietly, all the way to your bed.
[pause 8s]
Good night, {{osloveni|little one}}.
[pause 15s]
