## uvod
[soft] Lie down and get comfortable.
Snuggle into your blanket.
[pause 4s]
Your pillow is soft.
Your blanket is warm and heavy, like a gentle hug.
[pause 4s]
Now slowly breathe in.
Your tummy rises… up.
[pause 3s]
And slowly breathe out.
Your tummy sinks… down.
[pause 4s]
Once more. Breathe in… up.
[pause 3s]
And breathe out… down.
[pause 5s]
Your arms are resting.
Your legs are resting.
[pause 3s]
Your whole body is calm and heavy.
[pause 3s]
Your eyelids are closing all by themselves.
Let them stay closed.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight we are going to visit Goldenhair.
[pause 3s]
This is a story about kind Jiřík and the animals who help him.
[pause 4s]
Imagine you are standing on the seashore.
The sand is still warm from the sun.
[pause 3s]
Little waves roll slowly to the shore.
And roll away again.
In… and out.
[pause 5s]
Beside you sits a young man with kind eyes.
That is Jiřík.
[pause 2s]
He nods and smiles at you.
Come, let us go together.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] Jiřík works for an old king.
The king is tired and wants to rest.
[pause 3s]
One morning he says to Jiřík,
"Across the sea, in a glass castle, lives Goldenhair.
Her hair shines like the golden sun.
Go and invite her to visit us."
[pause 4s]
So Jiřík sets off.
On a brown horse, slowly.
Clip… clop… clip… clop…
[pause 5s]
The road leads through a meadow.
The grass smells sweet, and crickets chirp softly.
[pause 4s]
In the middle of the road is an anthill.
Ants are carrying seeds home.
Jiřík gently turns his horse around them.
[pause 3s]
"Thank you," whispers the ant queen.
"When you need us, we will help you."
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
The road leads through a forest.
Under a big oak sit two little ravens.
They hopped out of their nest to play, and now they are sleepy.
[pause 3s]
Jiřík lifts them in his warm hands.
He gently sets them back in their nest.
[pause 3s]
"Thank you," they croak softly.
"When you need us, we will help you."
[pause 5s]
Then Jiřík comes to the sea.
A little golden fish splashes in a puddle on the sand.
[pause 3s]
Jiřík lifts it gently into the sea.
The fish waves its fin.
"Thank you. When you need me, I will help you."
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Fishermen row Jiřík across the sea.
The boat rocks slowly.
Up… and down. Up… and down.
[pause 6s]
The wind is warm and smells of salt.
The seagulls doze on the rocks.
[pause 4s]
The oars dip into the sea, slow and quiet.
Splash…
[pause 2s]
Splash…
[pause 6s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
On the far shore stands the glass castle.
It glows in the evening sun like a lantern.
[pause 4s]
Goldenhair sits by the window, combing her hair.
Slowly. Strand by strand.
Every strand sparkles.
[pause 4s]
Her father, the gentle king of the castle, says,
"Goldenhair will go with you. But first, help us with three little things."
[pause 4s]
First, some pearls have spilled in the meadow.
Jiřík kneels in the grass.
And slowly the little ants arrive.
Each one carries a pearl.
Soon all the pearls are back on the string.
[pause 5s]
Next, Goldenhair's ring has dropped into the sea.
The golden fish swims up.
It holds the ring in its mouth.
[pause 5s]
Last, they need water from a faraway spring.
Two ravens fly in.
Each carries a tiny bottle.
The water is clear and cool as morning dew.
[pause 5s]
Goldenhair smiles.
"Thank you, Jiřík. I will gladly come with you."
[pause 4s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]

## dojezd
[slow] The boat carries them back across the sea.
The sun has set.
The stars come out.
[pause 6s]
Little waves lap against the boat.
Splash… splash…
[pause 6s]
Goldenhair rests her head on Jiřík's shoulder.
She closes her eyes.
[pause 7s]
[whisper] Jiřík closes his eyes too.
[pause 7s]
The sea is asleep.
The stars keep watch.
[pause 8s]
And Hajánek is already passing by.
Softly… on tiptoe…
[pause 8s]
Good night, {{osloveni|little one}}.
