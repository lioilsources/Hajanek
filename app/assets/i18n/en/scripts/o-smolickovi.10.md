## uvod
[soft] Lie down and snuggle into your blanket.
It is warm, like a little bird's nest.
[pause 3s]
Imagine a soft balloon in your tummy.
[pause 3s]
Breathe in. The balloon slowly fills up.
[pause 3s]
Breathe out. The balloon slowly shrinks.
[pause 4s]
Once more. Breathe in… and the balloon grows.
[pause 3s]
Breathe out… and the balloon is small again.
[pause 5s]
Your toes are resting.
Your knees are resting.
[pause 3s]
Your tummy is soft. Your shoulders are loose.
[pause 3s]
Your cheeks are calm, and your eyelids are heavy.
They can slowly close.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about Smolíček.
He is a little boy who lives in the forest with a deer.
[pause 3s]
It is a quiet story.
Everyone in it is friends.
[pause 4s]
Imagine you are in a forest clearing.
Sunlight shines through the leaves.
[pause 3s]
In the clearing stands a small wooden cottage.
On the doorstep sits a boy with messy hair.
That is Smolíček.
[pause 2s]
He smiles and scoots over to make room for you.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] Smolíček lives with a deer.
The deer has golden antlers.
In the sun, they sparkle like a crown.
[pause 4s]
Every morning, the deer goes to the meadow.
He grazes on sweet grass and clover.
[pause 3s]
"Play nicely, Smolíček," he says.
"I will be back for supper."
[pause 3s]
Smolíček nods.
And the deer walks slowly off between the trees.
[pause 5s]
Smolíček sweeps the cottage. Swish… swish… goes the broom.
Then he sits by the window and listens.
[pause 4s]
A woodpecker taps on a tree. Tap… tap…
[pause 3s]
Behind the cottage, a little brook bubbles.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Three little wood fairies come along the path.
They wear dresses of ferns, with wildflowers in their hair.
[pause 3s]
They are shy forest fairies.
They hide behind one another and giggle softly.
[pause 4s]
They knock on the door. Knock, knock.
"Smolíček, little fellow, open the door just a crack.
We have brought you strawberries."
[pause 4s]
Smolíček opens the door. Just a little.
The doorway smells of strawberries.
And three fairies smile at him.
[pause 4s]
"Come and play with us," they say softly.
"We will show you our clearing."
[pause 3s]
Smolíček closes the door and goes with them.
Along the soft path, slowly, step by step.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
The clearing is full of bluebells.
The fairies dance in a circle, and Smolíček dances too.
[pause 4s]
Then they sit in the grass.
They eat strawberries, sweet and warm from the sun.
[pause 4s]
The fairies sing a forest song.
It is quiet, like rustling leaves.
La la la… la la la…
[pause 5s]
The smallest fairy weaves a daisy crown for Smolíček.
[pause 2s]
She sets it on his head.
It smells of meadows and sunshine.
[pause 4s]
A bunny peeks out of the bushes and sits with them.
He wiggles his whiskers and listens.
[pause 5s]
The sun is setting.
[pause 3s]
The birds settle into their nests.
They give one more soft chirp.
[pause 4s]
Along the path comes the deer with golden antlers.
He knows Smolíček is playing with the fairies.
[pause 3s]
"Smolíček, it is time for supper," he says kindly.
He lowers his head, and Smolíček climbs onto his back.
[pause 4s]
The fairies wave. "We will come again."
[pause 4s]
The deer carries Smolíček through the forest. Step… step… step…
[pause 3s]
His fur is warm and smells of hay.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
In the cottage, they have supper.
Warm milk, bread with honey, and a few strawberries from the fairies.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]

## dojezd
[slow] Smolíček lies down in his bed of moss and hay.
[pause 5s]
The deer lies down by the door.
His golden antlers glow softly in the dark, like a little lamp.
[pause 6s]
The woodpecker is asleep.
Only the brook still bubbles quietly.
[pause 6s]
The fairies sleep in their clearing.
[pause 7s]
[whisper] Smolíček closes his eyes.
[pause 7s]
The whole forest is asleep.
[pause 8s]

Over the moss, light as a leaf.
