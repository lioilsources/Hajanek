## uvod
[soft] It is evening, and you are already in bed.
[pause 3s]
Your blanket warms you like a mug of cocoa.
Your pillow is round and soft.
[pause 4s]
Breathe in, like smelling a flower.
[pause 3s]
And breathe out, like blowing on a dandelion.
[pause 4s]
Once more. Smell the flower.
[pause 3s]
And blow on the dandelion. The fluff floats slowly away.
[pause 5s]
Now your toes are resting.
Your knees are resting.
[pause 3s]
Your tummy and shoulders are resting.
[pause 3s]
Your arms lie beside you, heavy and warm.
[pause 3s]
Your eyelids are so heavy, they close by themselves.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about Hansel and Gretel.
[pause 3s]
It is a sweet story about a gingerbread cottage.
Everyone in it is kind, and everything smells of gingerbread.
[pause 4s]
Imagine a forest path.
A little brook babbles beside it.
[pause 3s]
Plip… plop… goes the water over the pebbles.
[pause 3s]
On the path stand a brother and sister, Hansel and Gretel.
[pause 2s]
They are holding baskets for strawberries.
They wave for you to come along.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] Hansel and Gretel live with their father by the woods.
This morning, Father gave them two baskets.
[pause 3s]
"Pick some strawberries for the gingerbread granny," he said.
"Tonight you will sleep at her house."
[pause 4s]
So off you go. You, Hansel, and Gretel.
Slowly, along the soft path.
[pause 4s]
The brook comes with you.
Plip… plop…
[pause 4s]
Strawberries grow on the hillside.
They are small, red, and warm from the sun.
[pause 3s]
Gretel hands one to Hansel.
He chews it slowly.
It is sweet as honey.
[pause 4s]
The baskets are full and smell like summer.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
The sun sinks low over the trees.
Golden light glows between the trunks.
[pause 3s]
Then a sweet smell drifts by.
Honey. And cinnamon. And fresh gingerbread.
[pause 4s]
The path leads you to a little clearing.
In the middle stands a cottage.
[pause 3s]
The whole cottage is made of gingerbread.
There are gingerbread hearts on the roof.
The windows have white icing, like snow.
[pause 4s]
Hansel carefully breaks off a corner of the roof.
Crunch.
[pause 3s]
The gingerbread is soft and smells of honey.
Hansel shares it with Gretel. And with you.
[pause 4s]
A sleepy voice comes from inside,
"Well, well, someone is nibbling my gingerbread."
[pause 3s]
Gretel smiles and answers softly,
"It is only the breeze, Granny."
[pause 4s]
Someone laughs happily behind the door.
The door slowly opens.
[pause 3s]
In the doorway stands a small, round granny.
Her apron is covered in flour.
[pause 3s]
"Welcome, children," says the granny with a yawn.
"You tasted my roof. That is just fine.
Gingerbread is for eating."
[pause 4s]
Gretel gives her the basket of strawberries.
The granny smells them and hums happily.
[pause 4s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
Inside, the cottage is warm and dim.
Gingerbread cookies lie on the shelves.
Hearts. Stars. Little horses.
[pause 4s]
Grandpa is asleep in a rocking chair.
He has a checkered blanket on his knees.
[pause 3s]
Hmmm… phooo… hmmm… phooo…
[pause 4s]
The granny pours warm milk with honey.
[pause 3s]
Hansel and Gretel sip slowly.
The milk warms their tummies.
[pause 5s]
Then everyone gets a cookie and a little white icing.
Gretel draws a star on hers.
Hansel draws a sun.
[pause 4s]
The granny watches and smiles.
Her eyes slowly close.
"How lovely," she whispers.
[pause 5s]
Outside, the night is soft and still.
An owl hoots on a branch. Hoo… hoo…
The brook murmurs softly behind the cottage.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]
