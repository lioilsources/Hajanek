## uvod
[soft] The day is over, and your bed is waiting.
Snuggle under your blanket.
[pause 4s]
Your blanket is fluffy and smells clean.
[pause 3s]
Breathe in through your nose, slow and deep.
[pause 3s]
And breathe out, like cooling a warm spoon. Whooo…
[pause 4s]
Once more. A slow breath in…
[pause 3s]
And a slow puff on the spoon. Whooo…
[pause 5s]
Now your whole body is soft.
Soft as butter melting in warm porridge.
[pause 4s]
Soft arms. Soft legs. A soft tummy.
[pause 3s]
And your eyelids sink slowly, until they close.
[pause 5s]

## vstup
Tonight I will tell you about a magic little pot.
[pause 3s]
It is a cheerful story that smells sweet.
In it, everyone is full and happy.
[pause 4s]
Imagine a little village below a hill.
The houses have red roofs.
[pause 3s]
By a little cottage sits a girl with braids.
[pause 3s]
She swings her legs and smiles at you.
"Come gather pine cones with me," she says.
[pause 5s]

## pribeh
[soft] The girl lives with her mother in a little cottage.
They have little, but they love each other dearly.
[pause 3s]
You walk to the woods together.
She carries a basket and a slice of bread.
[pause 4s]
The woods smell of pine and mushrooms.
Pine cones lie in the moss like little hedgehogs.
She picks them one by one.
[pause 4s]
On a stump sits an old woman.
She is small and wrinkly, with kind eyes.
[pause 3s]
"I am hungry, dear child," she says softly.
[pause 2s]
The girl gives her the slice of bread.
All of it. And gladly.
[pause 4s]
The old woman eats slowly and smiles.
Then she takes a little clay pot out of her bundle.
[pause 3s]
"This is a magic pot," she says.
"Say, Cook, little pot, cook, and it will make sweet porridge.
Say, Stop, little pot, stop, and it will stop."
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
At home, the girl sets the pot on the table.
"Cook, little pot, cook," she says.
[pause 3s]
And the pot begins to cook.
It bubbles. Bloop… bloop… bloop…
[pause 4s]
The porridge is sweet and smells of milk and honey.
The girl and her mother eat and smile.
[pause 4s]
"Stop, little pot, stop," says the girl.
And the pot stops.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
The next day, you and the girl go to the woods again.
[pause 3s]
Mother stays home.
She would like some porridge.
[pause 3s]
"Cook, little pot, cook," says Mother.
Bloop… bloop… And the porridge cooks.
[pause 4s]
Mother eats until she is full.
But she cannot remember the other magic words.
[pause 3s]
So the pot keeps cooking.
The porridge spills over the top.
Slowly and softly, like a warm quilt.
[pause 4s]
It flows over the table, out the door, and into the yard.
[pause 3s]
Mother laughs.
She sits down in it, as if on a soft pillow.
[pause 4s]
The porridge flows on, across the village green.
It is warm, yellow, and fluffy.
[pause 4s]
A cat lies on it and purrs.
A dog rests his nose in it.
A hen sits on it, as if on a little nest.
[pause 4s]
The neighbors come outside.
They bring spoons and have a taste.
"Mmm, it is so sweet," they say, and laugh.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 5s]
That evening, you and the girl come home.
The whole village smells of milk and honey.
[pause 3s]
The porridge reaches the hill.
The girl smiles and says,
"Stop, little pot, stop."
[pause 4s]
And the pot stops cooking.
Bloop.
[pause 4s]
The whole village rests on a big porridge pillow.
It is warm and soft.
[pause 5s]
And everything is all right.
[pause 6s]

## dojezd
[slow] Mother tucks the girl into bed.
The little pot sits on the shelf, resting.
[pause 5s]
The neighbors yawn and go home to sleep.
[pause 6s]
The cat sleeps on the warm porridge pillow.
The dog sleeps beside her.
[pause 6s]
The windows go dark, one by one.
[pause 7s]
[whisper] The village smells of honey, and it sleeps.
[pause 7s]
The girl is asleep. Mother is asleep.
[pause 8s]
And Hajánek is already passing by.
Softly… on tiptoe…
[pause 8s]
Good night, {{osloveni|little one}}.
